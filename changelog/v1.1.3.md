## Added
- **Replies in the vault** — the vault's media viewer now shows the saved conversation: swipe left on a page or tap the new replies button (with a reply count) to read what was said about that image, and jump between saved media from the panel.
- **Download count stays visible** — while saves are running, a small badge in the corner of the media viewer keeps the count on screen after the controls fade out.

## Changed
- **Silent videos say so** — a video with no audio track now shows a greyed-out speaker labelled "This video has no audio" instead of a mute button that does nothing.
- **More reliable swipes** — a shorter, more deliberate sideways swipe opens replies or goes back in the media viewer; diagonal drags are left to the pager.
- **Vault viewer matches the thread viewer** — reply panels, swipe navigation and picture-in-picture behave the same in both places.

## Fixed
- **Vault swipe-to-replies did nothing** — the reply swipe in the vault viewer silently failed because the saved conversation loaded after the viewer opened; it now works once the posts are read.
- **Reply panel follows a shuffle** — when a shuffled vault viewer crosses into another thread, the reply panel now shows that thread's conversation instead of the previous one.
