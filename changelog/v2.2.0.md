## Added
- **Reverse image search** — search the current image with Google Lens, SauceNAO, IQDB, TinEye or Yandex from the viewer's menu, in threads and in the vault; a file that only exists on the phone can be shared to another app instead.
- **Search a video frame** — pick any frame of the current video with a slider and send it to a reverse image search; remote videos are fetched first.
- **Swipe between threads** — swipe sideways in a thread to reach the next or previous thread in the catalog you opened it from; back still returns to the catalog.
- **Text size and line spacing** — Settings > Reading has new rows for post text size and line spacing with a live preview; app chrome keeps the system size.
- **Expand images in the thread** — a new Settings > Media switch (off by default) makes a thumbnail tap show the full image in place; videos, gifs and sound posts still open the viewer, and data saver shows a Load pill first.
- **Cross-thread quote previews** — tapping a `>>>/board/no` quote opens the post in the preview sheet, with a "From /b/123" line, the copy it came from (live, saved or archived) and an Open thread button; a long press still opens the thread.
- **Tappable deadlinks** — a `>>123` whose post was pruned now opens in the preview sheet from a saved or archived copy of the thread when one has it.
- **Release notes in the updater** — Settings > Updates shows the release's notes as sections and bullets instead of a raw text block.

## Changed
- The Reading settings summary now shows text size, line spacing and history retention instead of the auto-refresh state.
- Video frames cut for a search share the cache with downloaded media, so it stays capped at the newest 20 files.
