## Added
- **Google Lens on local-only files.** Search image now sends a video frame or an imported file straight to Google Lens as a shared image, so you no longer need an online copy.

## Changed
- **Search with sheet.** The reason a file cannot go to every engine is said once under the title, and only the engines that need an online copy sit greyed out.
- **Sound filter in the vault.** With Videos selected in Saved, the sound toggle now gets its own line under the sort and filter chips instead of wrapping into the leftover width.
