## Added
- **Hold to save.** Long-press a thumbnail in a thread, or an open image or video in the viewer, to send it straight to the vault.
- **Replies saved with media.** Saving an image now keeps the text of every post it quotes and every reply to it, so the conversation is still readable after the thread is gone.
- **Vault Sync.** The refresh button in Saved now also fetches each saved thread's full comment section while it still exists, with a progress counter and a summary of what was refreshed or already gone.
- **Saved threads open offline.** When a thread has 404'd or you are offline, the media viewer falls back to the saved conversation instead of showing nothing.
- **Import your own files.** The Saved tab's + menu turns picked photos and videos, or a whole folder, into an offline thread you can browse like any other.
- **Double-tap to skip in videos.** Double-tap the left or right edge of a video to jump back or forward. The step is configurable and shrinks on short clips so it never overshoots.
- **Keep the screen on while watching.** The display stays awake while a video plays, with a switch to turn it off.
- **Settings for the new gestures.** Media & playback has switches for hold to save, double-tap skip, the skip step, and saving replies.

## Changed
- **Settings is now an index.** One row per section (Appearance, Reading, Media & playback, Boards, Links & privacy, Storage & data, Updates, About), each showing its current state, with the controls on their own subscreen.
- **Dev builds sit beside the release.** A development build installs as "Yotsuba dev" with its own data, and its Updates screen explains that updates go to the release build rather than claiming to be up to date.

## Removed
- **Thumbnail size and density settings.** They were stored but nothing in the app ever read them.
