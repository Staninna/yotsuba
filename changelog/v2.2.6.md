## Added
- **Reverse search for local files.** Search a video frame or an imported file with SauceNAO, IQDB, TinEye and Yandex, not just Lens: the sheet uploads it for you and opens the results.
- **Search local files by.** A new Privacy setting picks the route for those uploads, either the engine's own form or a temporary host that deletes the file after an hour.

## Changed
- **Progress in the search sheet.** The sheet stays open while an upload runs, shows which engine it is talking to, and offers "Try the temporary host" when a direct upload fails.
