## Added
- **Thread roulette.** A dice in the Home top bar opens a random thread from your favourite boards, and holding it picks which boards it may land on.
- **Board profiles.** Under Settings > Boards, one board can override text size, line spacing, autoplay, spoilers, inline images and thumbnail blur.
- **Your numbers.** Settings > About > Your numbers shows threads read, files saved, data fetched and busiest hour, all counted on the phone.
- **Copy text from an image.** The viewer menu reads the text in a picture on-device and copies it.
- **Export a video as an animated WebP.** The viewer turns the opening seconds of a webm into a sticker other apps take.
- **Loop a section.** A webm loops between two handles on the seek bar instead of over the whole clip.
- **Re-download missing files.** The vault marks files deleted outside the app and fetches them back from the thread or an archive.
- **Post translation.** Turn on Translate posts in Reading and the post menu offers an on-device translation.

## Changed
- **More archives.** A dead thread is now looked up on 4plebs, fireden, palanq and archived.moe as well, in order, until one has it.
- **Catalog sort.** Each board keeps its own sort: bump order, newest, most replies, most images or replies per hour.
- **Post time.** Reading lets a post show its time as relative, as a date and time, or both, in your zone or the board's.
- **Catalog badges.** A visited thread shows how many replies landed since you read it, and threads that quote each other say so.
- **Blurred thumbnails.** Catalog thumbnails can stay blurred until tapped, globally or per board.
- **Pure black.** The dark theme has a true black option for OLED screens.
- **Fade filter.** A filter can dim a match to 40% instead of removing it, so the thread still reads around it.
- **Reply tree.** The quote preview lists replies as a tree, each branch folding under its own line.

## Fixed
- **Undo after a swipe.** Swiping a row away and tapping Undo puts it back instead of deleting it a second time.
- **Deep links.** A link to a post opens on that post rather than at the top of the thread, and a cold start no longer lands on the wrong screen.
- **Sharing a missing file.** Sharing or exporting a vault file that is gone from disk no longer crashes.
- **Clear cache.** Clearing caches now empties the video cache too.
- **Scroll positions.** The catalog grid and the boards list come back where you left them.
- **Zoom.** Photos and videos pinch to 5x instead of stopping at 2x.
- **Stats wording.** A one-day streak reads "1 day", and a board code the profile field rejects now says why.
