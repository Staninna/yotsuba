## Added
- **Home tab.** Your favourite boards open as swipeable pages with a tab strip you can reorder by long-press and drag, or drop on the strip to unfavourite.
- **Watched threads widget.** A home-screen widget lists your bookmarked threads with unread counts, refreshes in the background, and opens the thread on tap.
- **Content filters.** Settings > Filters hides or stubs posts and catalog threads by subject, comment, name, tripcode, flag, poster ID or filename, plain or regex, per board.
- **Backup and restore.** Bookmarks, hidden threads and settings export automatically to a file in the vault. A fresh install offers to restore them.
- **Archive fallback.** A thread that has 404ed on 4chan loads from desuarchive or arch.b4k.co where the board is covered, or from your vault copy when offline.
- **Duplicate finder.** The vault can hash every saved file and show exact and near-duplicate groups with a suggested keeper. Saves already in the vault are skipped.
- **Thread snapshots.** Bookmarked threads have their posts written to the vault in the background so the text survives the thread dying, with an optional prune of dead sidecars.
- **Vault statistics, gallery and tree view.** A stats sheet charts vault size per board and week, threads get a media gallery with save-all, and posts can be viewed nested under their parent.

## Changed
- **Threads tab.** Bookmarks and History merge into one tab with a Watched/Recent switch. Bookmarks can be sorted, pinned and pruned, and history has a search field.
- **Quote previews.** Tapping a `>>123` link opens a bottom-sheet preview that reads as a mini thread. A Reading setting makes tap jump instead.
- **Media viewer.** Sound posts play their audio track, failed videos show a retry, a Data saver setting defers heavy media on metered connections, and the top bar gains a replies button.
- **Vault explorer.** Search by file name or subject, sort and filter from a chip row, multi-select for delete, share and save to gallery, and rename or merge imported threads.
- **Bookmarks.** Refresh runs in the background on a schedule you pick, one read mark replaces the two unread counters, and new replies post a notification.
- **Motion and haptics.** Lists, floating controls and skeletons animate. Long presses, saves, deletes and jumps buzz. A Reduce motion switch turns all of it off.
- **Links.** Thread and catalog links from a browser, a share sheet or the widget open in the app. The settings gear sits in every tab's top bar.
- **Settings.** Stored as one blob and migrated in place. Hidden threads moved from Storage to Boards. New switches for vault-delete confirmation, snapshots, pruning and data saver.

## Fixed
- **Bookmark read mark.** Rises from the bottom visible post instead of resetting when the thread opens, so the unread count stays right.
- **Thread polling.** Stops while the app is in the background and for closed threads. A failed refresh keeps the loaded thread on screen.
- **History.** Undo puts a removed row back with its read mark intact, clear-all is disabled when empty, and a lone slash no longer matches every entry.
- **Updater.** Cancelling the system installer no longer re-shows it.
- **Board search.** Board codes rank above title matches and sections order by their best hit.
- **Video stills.** Saved videos get a local first frame and duration so the grid stops re-fetching thumbnails.
- **Post text.** Out-of-range numeric entities render as literal text instead of crashing the parser.

## Removed
- **Auto Backup.** Android's cloud backup is off so local data stays on the device. Use the in-app backup instead.
