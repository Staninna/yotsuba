## Added
- **Trash in the vault.** Deleted files now sit in Trash under the vault's More menu for a week, so you can restore one long after the undo snackbar is gone.
- **Collapse read posts.** Opening a watched thread with new replies folds everything up to your read mark into one "N earlier posts" row you can tap to expand, with a switch for it under Settings > Reading.

## Changed
- **Duplicate finder scope.** Inside a board or thread the finder now looks only at what is on screen, and it rereads the vault from disk first so files changed by a sync are counted.
- **Watched list sort order.** The sort you pick in the Threads tab sticks across leaving the tab and restarting the app.

## Fixed
- **Lock screen.** Coming back from the lock screen returns you to where you were instead of the Home tab.
- **Unread counts after a restore.** Restoring a backup fetches the watched threads right away, so bookmarks no longer read 0 unread until the next refresh.
- **Trash across restarts.** Deleted files survive the app being killed rather than vanishing for good on the next launch.
