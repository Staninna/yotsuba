## Added
- **App lock** — Settings > Privacy can require your phone's unlock (fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern) when you open Yotsuba, with a "Lock again after" delay of right away, 30 s, 1 min or 5 min.
- **Hide saved media from the gallery** — a switch in Settings > Storage keeps the Yotsuba folder out of Gallery and Photos without moving any files.
- **Thread gallery filters** — the thread gallery has chips for All, Images, Videos, With sound and Silent, and "Save all" now saves only what the filter shows.
- **Sound filter in the vault** — with Videos selected in Saved, a second toggle narrows to videos with sound or silent ones, and the shuffle menu gains "Videos with sound" and "Silent videos".
- **Sound badge on saved videos** — vault tiles show a speaker icon when the video plays with sound.
## Changed
- **Video tiles in the thread gallery** — videos now carry a play badge so they can be told apart from images.
- **Board hiding** — hiding or showing a whole category now acts on the boards in it, and a board 4chan adds to a hidden category later shows up until you hide it.
- **Wording** — error, offline and update messages were reworded.
## Fixed
- **Rescan fills in sound info** — a rescan of the vault now records whether each saved video has sound, so the sound filters work on older saves.
- **Faster duplicate checks** — looking up an already-saved file by hash no longer scans the whole vault index.
