## Fixed
- **App Lock prompts again when you come back.** If the system dismissed the unlock prompt (face unlock timed out, or you switched away with it open), returning to a locked Yotsuba shows it again instead of leaving you on a locked screen with only the Unlock button.
- **Cancelling the PIN screen no longer bounces you back into it.** Backing out of the unlock on Android 8.x waits for your next return before asking again.
