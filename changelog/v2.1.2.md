## Fixed
- **App Lock prompts again when you come back** — if the unlock prompt was dismissed by the system (face unlock timed out, or you switched away with it open), returning to a locked Yotsuba now shows it again instead of leaving you on a locked screen with only the Unlock button.
- **Cancelling the PIN screen no longer bounces you back into it** — backing out of the unlock on Android 8.x waits for your next return before asking again.
