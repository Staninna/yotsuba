## Added
- **Boards, catalog and threads.** Browse every 4chan board, grouped by category, with favourites, search and per-board or per-category hiding. Open a catalog in comfortable, compact or list layout. Threads render greentext, spoilers, quotelinks and reply previews.
- **Media viewer.** Swipe vertically through a thread's images and videos, pinch to zoom, keep videos looping or auto-advancing, watch in picture-in-picture, and swipe left on a post to read its replies without leaving the viewer.
- **Saved media vault.** Save files to `/sdcard/Yotsuba/<board>/<thread>/`, browse them by board and thread from the Saved tab, shuffle-play everything, and rescan the folder to rebuild the index after a reinstall.
- **Bookmarks with unread counts.** Bookmark a thread and the Bookmarks tab shows "+N" new replies and unread pills. It refreshes each time you open it.
- **History.** Threads you read are grouped by Today, Yesterday and This week, reopen at the post you left off on, and can be kept forever, 30 days or 7 days.
- **In-app updates.** The Updates section in Settings checks GitHub for a newer release and downloads and installs it in place.
- **Auto-refresh and in-thread search.** Open threads poll for new posts and mark them with a "N new posts" divider. A search bar steps through matching posts.
- **Link safety and offline reading.** External links show a confirmation with an "always trust this domain" option. Cached boards and catalogs still open when you are offline.
