## Added
- **Boards, catalog and threads** — browse every 4chan board (grouped by category, with favourites, search and per-board or per-category hiding), open a catalog in comfortable, compact or list layout, and read threads with greentext, spoilers, quotelinks and reply previews.
- **Media viewer** — swipe vertically through a thread's images and videos, pinch to zoom, keep videos looping or auto-advancing, watch in picture-in-picture, and swipe left on a post to read its replies without leaving the viewer.
- **Saved media vault** — save files to `/sdcard/Yotsuba/<board>/<thread>/`, browse them by board and thread from the Saved tab, shuffle-play everything, and rescan the folder to rebuild the index after a reinstall.
- **Bookmarks with unread counts** — bookmark a thread and see "+N" new replies and unread pills on the Bookmarks tab; it refreshes on its own each time you open it.
- **History** — threads you read are grouped by Today, Yesterday and This week, reopen at the post you left off on, and can be kept forever, 30 days or 7 days.
- **In-app updates** — the Updates section in Settings checks GitHub for a newer release and downloads and installs it in place.
- **Auto-refresh and in-thread search** — open threads poll for new posts with a "N new posts" divider, and a search bar steps through matching posts.
- **Link safety and offline reading** — external links show a confirmation with an "always trust this domain" option, and cached boards and catalogs still open when you're offline.
